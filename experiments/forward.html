EXPERIMENT THEORY

Understanding Forward Bias

What is Forward Bias?

A diode is said to be forward biased when its anode is connected to the positive terminal and its cathode is connected towards the negative terminal of the DC supply.

Working Principle

When the forward voltage increases, the potential barrier of the diode decreases. After the knee region, the diode current increases rapidly.

Important Observation

A silicon diode typically has a forward voltage around 0.7 V under common operating conditions. The exact value depends on current and temperature.
