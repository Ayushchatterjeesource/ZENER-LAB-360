Load Regulation Principle

A Zener diode is connected in reverse bias across the load. A series resistor limits the supply current. When load resistance changes, the Zener current changes to help maintain the output voltage, provided the diode remains within its regulation region.

EXPERIMENT THEORY

Understanding Load Regulation

What is Load Regulation?

Load regulation describes the ability of a power supply or voltage regulator to maintain its output voltage when the load changes.

Working Principle

A Zener diode connected in reverse breakdown can maintain an approximately constant voltage across the load. When load current changes, the Zener current adjusts accordingly within its permitted operating range.

Role of Potentiometer

A potentiometer can be used as an adjustable resistance in the experimental arrangement to vary the load condition and study the output voltage response.

Load Regulation Formula

Percentage Load Regulation ((VNL − VFL) / VFL) × 100%

VNL represents the no-load output voltage and VFL represents the full-load output voltage. Always use the convention prescribed by your laboratory manual when calculating and reporting load regulation.
