EXPERIMENT THEORY

Understanding Reverse Bias

What is Reverse Bias?

A diode is reverse biased when its cathode is connected towards the positive terminal and its anode towards the negative terminal of the DC supply.

Reverse Leakage Current

Before breakdown, only a very small reverse current flows through the diode. This current is called reverse leakage current.

Zener Breakdown

When the reverse voltage reaches the breakdown voltage, the reverse current increases significantly. A Zener diode is designed to operate in this region within its rated limits.
