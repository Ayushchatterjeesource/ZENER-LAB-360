ABOUT THE PROJECT

A New Way to Experience Electrical Engineering

ZENER-LAB-360 is an educational virtual laboratory project designed to help students understand the practical study of Zener diode V-I characteristics and load regulation.

It combines interactive 3D visualization, circuit learning, measurement concepts and simulation in a single learning environment.
